# **6. Risk Management**

- Impact: H = milestone deliverable not possible, M = deliverable delay·quality drop, L = avoidable

- Mitigation priority: H×H → immediate Spike, H×M or M×H → next Sprint, L×H or H×L → backlog, otherwise → monitor
